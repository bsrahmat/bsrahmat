### Prof. Basuki Rahmat is a Professor specializing in Intelligent IoT, control systems, robotics, and internet-based engineering laboratories. His work focuses on developing scalable and accessible remote laboratory solutions, such as the Internet-based Temperature Control Lab (iTCLab) and Motor Control Lab (iMCLab), aimed at democratizing engineering education globally.

He has also developed innovative robotic systems including the Bela Negara robots and Craft Enterprise Robot (CER), contributing to applied robotics for community empowerment. His research integrates cyber-physical systems, IoT, and AI to enable accessible, impactful, and globally deployable engineering education platforms.
